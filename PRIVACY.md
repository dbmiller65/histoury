# Privacy Policy for Histoury

Last updated: September 2026

Histoury is designed to collect as little information about you as possible.

## What We Collect

- **Location**: When you tap "My Current Location," Histoury uses your device's GPS to determine where you are, so it can look up a historical fact about that place. If you type in a place name instead, no location data is used at all.

## How Your Location Is Used

Your location (or the place name you typed) is sent, at the moment you request a fact, to Histoury's own backend service, solely to generate the historical fact you asked for. It is not logged, stored, sold, or shared with any third party, and it is not used for advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

## Text-to-Speech

When you tap the speaker icon to hear a fact read aloud, Histoury uses your device's own built-in text-to-speech engine. This happens entirely on your device — no text or audio is sent anywhere for this feature.

## Maps

The map shown on the home screen uses Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android) to display your location, via the standard mapping service built into your device's operating system.

## What We Don't Do

- We don't require an account or collect any personal identifiers (name, email, etc.)
- We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs
- We don't track you across other apps or websites
- We don't sell or share your data with anyone

## Children's Privacy

Histoury is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
